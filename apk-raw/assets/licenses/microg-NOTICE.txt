HttpFormClient/CheckinClient, LoginActivity.java, ConsentSignInActivity.kt,
auth/signin/extensions.kt, auth.proto, checkin.proto, deviceconfig.proto.

Modifications: a private host-process content provider replaces the system
authenticator. Account credentials use AndroidKeyStore encrypted storage.
Virtual guest callers use the cloned APK's package and signing certificate.
Only the authentication/check-in and consent subset is included. No separate
microG installation is required, and this is not a complete GmsCore build.
